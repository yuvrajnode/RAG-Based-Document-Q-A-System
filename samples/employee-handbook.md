# Northwind Logistics - Employee Handbook

Revision 7, effective 1 March 2026. Supersedes revision 6.

## Working hours

Standard hours are 09:00 to 17:30, Monday to Friday, with an unpaid hour for
lunch. Core hours are 10:00 to 16:00; outside those, start and finish times are
flexible provided the weekly total reaches 37.5 hours.

Hybrid staff are expected on site two days a week. Depot and warehouse roles are
on site full time because of equipment access.

## Annual leave

Full-time employees accrue 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, in
addition to public holidays. Leave accrues monthly at 2.083 days and is
pro-rated for part-time staff.

Up to five unused days may be carried into the following year and must be taken
before 31 March. Anything beyond five days is forfeited unless the line manager
recorded a business reason in Workday before 15 December.

Requests of five days or more need 21 days notice. Requests during the December
peak require director approval.

## Sick leave and absence

Report absence to your line manager before 09:30 on the first day. Self
certification covers the first seven calendar days; a fit note is required from
day eight. Company sick pay runs at full pay for 20 working days in any rolling
twelve month period, then statutory pay only.

## Expenses

Expenses are submitted in Concur using form EX-114 within 60 days of the spend.
Claims older than 60 days are rejected automatically and need a director
override to reinstate.

Receipts are required for every claim above 25 pounds. Mileage is reimbursed at
45 pence per mile for the first 10,000 miles in a tax year and 25 pence
thereafter. Standard class rail is the default; first class needs prior written
approval.

Client entertainment must be pre-approved by the account director and coded to
cost centre CC-4400.

## Equipment

Laptops are replaced on a three year cycle. Faults go to the service desk under
reference prefix SD-, for example SD-2200; the target response is four working
hours during core hours.

Personal devices may access company email through Intune enrolment only.
Removable storage is blocked on all managed endpoints.

## Probation and notice

Probation is six months for permanent roles, reviewed at three and six months.
Notice during probation is one week either way. After probation, notice is one
month for grades 1 to 4 and three months for grades 5 and above.
